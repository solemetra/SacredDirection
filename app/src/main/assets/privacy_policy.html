This Privacy Policy applies to the Sacred Direction mobile application (the “Application”), provided as a free service “AS IS”.

Information We Use

The Application needs location only to:

  • show your position on the map and the direction to Qibla;
  • calculate prayer times for your area.

When you allow location access, the device may provide precise GPS coordinates and/or approximate location (for example via Wi‑Fi or mobile network), depending on your settings and Android version.

The Application does not use the device magnetometer or compass sensor.

Map display: to show the map, the Application may download map tiles from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org). That request goes from your device to OpenStreetMap’s servers (typically your IP address and which map area is requested). We do not receive that data on our servers.

Local storage on your device: latitude, longitude, and app settings (for example prayer calculation options) are stored in the Application’s private storage on your phone so the app, widget, and alarms can work offline. We do not operate our own servers to collect this data.

The Application does not use your data for advertising or marketing and does not sell personal data.

Third Parties

We do not send your location or prayer settings to the Developer’s servers.

Third-party services involved only as described above (for example OpenStreetMap for map tiles). We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the Application.

Your Choices

  • You can decline location permission; Qibla on the map and automatic prayer times for your current position will not work fully until you allow access or set location another way if the app offers it.
  • You can revoke location permission anytime in your device Settings → Apps → Sacred Direction → Permissions.
  • You can stop all use of the Application by uninstalling it; local data is removed with the app.

Questions or requests about privacy: solemetra@gmail.com.

Data Retention

Data stays on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the Application. The Developer does not keep a copy on external servers.

Children

The Application is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has contacted us, email solemetra@gmail.com.

Security

We rely on your device’s security. Because we do not host user databases, there is no central server copy of your location to breach.

Changes

We may update this Policy. The current version is always on this page. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated Policy.

Consent

By tapping Agree in the Application (or by using the Application after reading this Policy), you consent to processing as described above.
