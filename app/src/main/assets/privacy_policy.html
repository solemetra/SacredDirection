Sacred Direction Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Sacred Direction application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application") for mobile devices, created by Bayburin Elvir (hereinafter referred to as the "Developer") as a free service. This service is provided "AS IS".

Effective date: September 23, 2025

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects minimal information necessary for its operation, including:

  • Precise geolocation data (GPS coordinates).
  • Approximate geolocation data (IP addresses and MAC addresses of available Wi-Fi devices).
  • Device magnetometer data.
  • This data is used exclusively to determine the Qibla direction and calculate prayer times. The Application does not collect data for advertising, marketing, or commercial purposes and does not transfer it to third parties.

Third-Party Access

The Application does not transfer any data, including geolocation or magnetometer information, to third parties. The data is processed exclusively on the user's device and is not saved on the Developer's servers.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the Application. You may use the standard uninstall processes available on your mobile device or through the mobile application marketplace.

Data Retention Policy

The Application does not retain any user data. All calculations are performed locally on the device, and the data is not transferred or stored after the function is used.

Age Restrictions

This Application has an age rating of 18+ and is not intended for use by anyone under this age. The Developer does not knowingly collect any data from children or individuals under the age of 18. If you are a parent or guardian and you become aware that your child has provided any data through the Application, please contact us at solemetra@gmail.com so that we can take the necessary actions.

Security

The Developer relies on the device's built-in security features to protect data. Since data is not transferred or stored, the risk of a data leak is non-existent.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The Developer will notify you of any changes by updating this page. You are advised to consult this policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.

Your Consent

By using the Application, you consent to the processing of your data for the specified purposes as set forth in this Privacy Policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, please contact the Developer via email at solemetra@gmail.com.
